# Succinct plots

- Aim for plots that quickly tell you what you need to get out of your data

- 🧩🚀 Figures should stand on their own (_standalone_)

- For inspiration look at the figure below from the [paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41537-021-00191-y)

![Plot](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41537-021-00191-y/figures/4)

- I have used arrows to guide the user (_the arrows represent a subset of patients who are at high risk_)

- Divergent colour palette

- A figure caption that explains everything the user needs to know and the interpretation

- Ideally the reader should not have to read the main text/report at all!
